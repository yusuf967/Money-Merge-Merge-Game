PRIVACY NOTICE

Notice under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Data controller

The developer of Money Merge, Yusuf Doğan (App Store: Yusuf Doan), is the data controller. Contact: doganyusuf967@gmail.com.

2. Game data on your device

Your stage progress, stars, gems, wealth, scores, empire, settings and language choice are stored only on this device. There is no account and no cloud save; the developer has no server and cannot see this data. Purpose: running the game. Legal ground: performance of the service you asked for (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Deleting the app removes all of it.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, USA). When an ad loads, your IP address, device model, operating system and language, app information and ad interactions are sent to Google, which processes them on servers abroad to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. This is a transfer of personal data abroad (KVKK Art. 9). Your game data is not sent to Google.

Your advertising ID (IDFA) and personalised ads are used only if you tap "Allow" in the iOS tracking prompt AND "Personalised Ads" is on in Settings; the legal ground is your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1 and 9). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings or in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Otherwise only non-personalised ads are shown. In the EEA and the UK, Google's consent form is shown first.

4. Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center on iPhone, your total stars are sent with your Game Center nickname to Apple's leaderboard (Apple Inc. and its affiliates, servers abroad), where other players can see them. Your nickname and profile belong to your Apple account and are managed by Apple; signing out of Game Center stops this. Legal ground: providing the leaderboard (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). To report an offensive nickname, contact Apple or write to doganyusuf967@gmail.com; we review reports and forward them to Apple when needed.

5. Notifications and purchases

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no server is used and nothing is sent to anyone. They arrive only if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off in Settings > Notifications or in your phone settings. There are no real-money purchases in the game.

6. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data processed by Google and Apple is kept for the periods in their own privacy policies.

7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it is transferred to, to request correction or deletion and that these be notified to those third parties, to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.
